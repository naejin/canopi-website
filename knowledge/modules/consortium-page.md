# Responsibility

The consortium tool's page: the visitor picks a climate and gets 6 to 12 plants at once (one plant list per climate, format 3); the country is optional and only sets the invasive warnings. The plants that work together, shown as layers, over time and by job, with Swap, Add, details, share and print. All logic comes from the [Consortium core](/modules/consortium-core.md); the page never copies its rules.

# Behaviour worth knowing

- After a climate is chosen, focus moves to `#ct-result-title` and `#ct-say` announces the summary; on phone the heading is scrolled to the top so the drawing and its numbered key show on the first screen.
- The warning box, headed "With a warning", is the same at every size (C5, scale-b fix): one line per kind in the design-scale §2.6 order (invasive here, toxic, not suited to your site, shared pests, invasive in n countries (with no country: n is the distinct countries the plants on the line are recorded invasive in, plural in all 11 locales, never "somewhere", C4), not checked yet), each with its count, up to three names and "Show n", which sets the list's Warning filter (and opens the list). "Not checked yet" carries `att.uncheckedNote`; no card has a "not checked yet" chip (the details say it). Slight toxicity stays a soft chip on the card.
- One warning heading everywhere (owner, 2026-10-28): `consortium.withWarning` ("With a warning" and its translations) heads the flagged plants in Swap and Add (with `flaggedNote` under it), the warnings summary, the print table's warnings column and the hidden label of the warning mark on the drawing, its key and the Over time rows. No "Cautions" label: the merged soft chip shows its list alone, first letter capitalised (`capFirst`).
- Swap and Add list flagged plants last, under "With a warning" (`withWarning`); choosing one asks one confirmation that names the warnings.
- No count of free places anywhere (decision "Scale mockup approved"): the summary reads "{n} plants · {room} · {n} kept"; the room hints name the height and width the room check allows and the suggestion size, from the core ("Plants up to 6 m tall and across · suggests 6 plants", "… 12 m … · suggests 9 plants", "Plants of any size · suggests 12 plants"; every locale names plants); the room never limits how many plants a consortium has: on a result a room change only flags the plants that no longer fit (no re-suggestion), and Fill fills up to the room's suggestion size; the "Fill the gaps" row shows only when `fillable()` finds a plant to add, else the "I want to grow…" search sits there. The ledes say a consortium starts at 6 to 12 plants and can grow to hundreds.
- Main crops (scale chunk A, 2026-10-28): after a climate is picked (cards, or a link with only `c`), the status `crops` asks "What do you mainly want to grow?" (optional): a search over the climate's food plants (`crops.js`), up to three chips with 44 px remove buttons, a `role=status` line (`#crop-st`) for matches, the limit and no-match (naming another climate's list when one has the plant, with no switch offered), one-tap examples, and one Suggest button ("Suggest plants around it/them", hint "To get a suggestion without it/them, remove…"), no Skip. The crops become kept members; the result shows "Built around the main crop(s): …" with Change crops (back to the step with "Back to your consortium"; suggesting again keeps the other kept plants, with Undo). A main crop is tagged "Main crop" on cards, rows, details and print; its details have a Main crop section and "What it does", and its foot offers Keep, "Not a main crop" and Remove (no Swap). The crop step is never saved; a reload returns to the result or the climate.
- Add anyway: the Add search lists plants only other climates have under "In another climate's list" (their files loaded on demand, `loadOthers`) with "Add anyway"; such a plant is kept, labelled "from the {Climate} list" (`list.<climate>` strings) on rows, cards, details (a section explaining where its data comes from) and print, and travels in the link's `g`.
- Side view (scale chunk C, 2026-10-28, owner rows "one side-view drawing at every size", "warn, never hide"; review fixes the same day under coordinator rulings): `sideMarkup(flags, print)` draws the four bands always (an empty one says `emptyLayer`), each with its name and light words, on one log scale. The cue above reads "Layers are about light, not height…" (`lightCue`, §2.3). Up to 20 plants each plant is a `plantSVG` silhouette with its number (a dot when it has a warning; 13 to 20 plants on a phone alternate two rows of 19 px numbers) and a numbered key with heights (`sideHeight`: `heightText`, the one height helper, with "climbing or trailing" beside a climber's recorded size (`sv.vineH`, review W1 ruling: the data's height can be a runner's length, such as Pumpkin 12 m, so the site never says "climbs to"); `heightRangeText` in details); the caption adds `sv.dashedNote` and `sv.climbNote` when they apply. The phone plot is 88 px, desktop 72 px, print 56 px. Above 20 each plant is one bar mark (`.sv-marks i`, `--h` its true height, tallest first) in one shade per band that reaches 3:1 against the surface in light and dark (`--m-*`), with a 1 px surface-coloured gap; only where the marks are under 3 px apart (`svFit` after each render and on resize, side.js `denseAt`) do they touch and alternate with a second shade (`--m2-*`), and the caption then shows `sv.denseNote`. Warning dots are never wider than a mark. Under each band: its count, a sentence from `bandFacts` (`sv.all`, `sv.most`, `sv.tall`, `sv.short`, `sv.climb` "Also 2 climbing or trailing plants: Pumpkin 12 m, …", `sv.unknown`; lists use one separator, "and n more" past 3, `namedList`) and "Show n" (sets the Layer filter). A band with marks is one tab stop (`role=group`, named by its count, `aria-describedby` its sentence; no arrow-key instruction, since screen readers in browse mode never pass the arrows; its 1, 5 and 20 m labels and its tip are `aria-hidden`, review W3); an empty band is an image with no tab stop. Hover, focus and, for sighted keyboard users, the arrow keys, Home and End name a mark in a tooltip and through `#ct-say`; a key calls `preventDefault` only when it moves to another mark, so Page Up/Down and an arrow at the end still scroll. A plant with no height is dashed at its layer's usual height. `sideViewHTML` returns a placeholder when the markup has not changed and `placeSide()` moves the old node back, so a re-render never rebuilds the drawing. Print at 20 plants or fewer draws the same view (no tab stops); above 20 print is state 13: At a glance (`glanceHTML`: plants and plants with a warning per layer and in all, then the kinds of warning from `warnKindList`, shared with the warning box), then the table, no marks.
- Numbers and units: in every locale a no-break space (U+00A0) joins a number, a placeholder or a plural count to its unit in the `consortium.*` strings (`meters`, `sv.most`, the room hints, the stage years…), so "0.3" and "m" never wrap apart (review W2; `consortium-side.test.mjs` checks all 11 locales). Tests read page text with spaces folded, but attributes keep the U+00A0.
- Phone: the side view is the same drawing, each band's name above its strip and the 5 m line unlabelled; Keep and Remove are icon buttons with labels; the "I want to grow…" search sits under the views when no place is free.
- A known climate opens on the result unless the crop step comes first (`opensOnResult`: a save, or a link with `a`, a non-empty `p`, `x`, `n` or `g`, or a guide plant the list has): the inline first-paint script in ConsortiumPage.astro follows the app's start rules (the link's `c`, else its `climate`, when the link has `c`, `climate` or `plant`; else the saved consortium's `c`; else `html[data-climate]`, checked against `data-climates` on `#ct-head`) and sets `data-step="1"` and the compact head before paint; the country never holds the setup. `#ct-app` keeps `min-height: 100vh` while loading, so nothing shifts.
- A confirmation for a flagged plant replaces that plant's Add or Use button, inside its own list row (never two Add buttons, and a list holds only items); Cancel gives the focus back to the button.
- A double click on Remove removes one plant (`repeatClick`: the second click of a double click, or a pointer click within 500 ms, is ignored; keyboard presses always count).
- Closing a sheet from an action (Swap, Remove in details, Add, a new climate) closes the dialog at once and moves focus to the plant, Undo, or the result title.
